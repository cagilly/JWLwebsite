What is a property valuation?

A property valuation is a professional assessment of what a property would sell for on the open market at a specific date. It is not an asking price, and it is not an estate agent's estimate of what might be achievable with the right campaign. It is a considered figure, supported by evidence, that can be relied upon for a financial or legal decision.

Our valuations are prepared to European Valuation Standards and set out not only the figure but the comparable sales, market conditions and property characteristics that produced it.

When you need one

Common reasons clients instruct us

  • Buying or selling and wanting an independent view free of any sales agenda
  • Applying for or restructuring a loan secured on property
  • Transferring property between family members or dissolving joint ownership
  • Establishing a value for tax purposes, including Capital Acquisitions Tax and Capital Gains Tax
  • Insurance, accounting, or company and pension asset valuation
  • Settling a dispute where an impartial figure is needed
The deliverable

What your valuation includes

[To be confirmed against a sample report, see note below.]

  • A full internal and external inspection of the property
  • A written report stating the open market value as at a defined date
  • The comparable sales evidence the valuation is based on
  • Commentary on the property's condition, accommodation and any factors materially affecting value
  • A report signed by a PSRA-licensed, IPAV-registered valuer holding TEGoVA REV accreditation

Why choose an independent valuer?

JWL does not sell property. We produce a figure and we show you the evidence for it. That is the entire service.

An estate agent valuing your home is, in most cases, also pitching for the instruction to sell it. There is nothing improper about that, but it does mean the figure serves two purposes at once. When the valuation has to satisfy a lender, a solicitor or Revenue, that ambiguity is a problem.

Questions

Property valuation FAQs

How much does a property valuation cost in Dublin?

It depends on the property and the type of report required. Tell us what you need and we will come back to you with a fee before anything starts.

How long does the inspection take?

Typically 30 to 60 minutes for a standard residential property. Larger or more complex properties take longer.

How long is a valuation valid for?

A valuation states a value at a specific date. In a moving market most lenders and institutions will treat a report as current for around three to six months, though the receiving party sets its own requirement. If yours has expired, we can usually update it more quickly and economically than starting fresh.
