What goes into a JWL Property Valuation

Three things, in every valuation we issue.

Judgement

More than ten years valuing property across Dublin and Leinster. We know how a particular road or estate actually trades, rather than what an online estimate or index suggests.

Worth

A figure grounded in the market as it stands: recent sales of comparable properties, current conditions, and the particular qualities of the property itself.

Legitimacy

Professional qualifications and recognised valuation standards behind the report, so it is accepted by the lender, solicitor or authority who has to rely on it.

That is what we mean by evidence before opinion.

How a JWL valuation works

01

Get in touch

Tell us about the property and what the valuation is for. The purpose shapes the report, so we ask up front. Call, email, or use the form.

02

Inspection

We arrange a time that suits and inspect the property in person.

03

Evidence and analysis

We assemble the comparable evidence (recent sales of genuinely similar properties in the area), and apply professional valuation practice and experience to reach a figure.

04

Your valuation

Issued in the format the receiving party requires: to you, or directly to your lender, solicitor or the relevant authority, whichever suits.
