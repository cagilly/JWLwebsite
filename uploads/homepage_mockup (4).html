What goes into a JWL Property Valuation

Three things, in every valuation we issue.

Judgement

More than ten years valuing property across Dublin and Leinster — knowing how a particular road or estate actually trades, not what an online estimate or index suggests.

Worth

A figure grounded in the market as it stands: recent sales of comparable properties, current conditions, and the particular qualities of the property itself.

Legitimacy

Professional accreditation and recognised valuation standards behind the report — so it is accepted by the lender, solicitor or authority who has to rely on it.

That is what we mean by evidence before opinion.
