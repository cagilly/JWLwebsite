Services/Local Authority Valuation

Local Authority Home Loan Valuations in Dublin and Leinster

Valuation reports prepared to local authority requirements, for Local Authority Home Loans, Purchase and Renovation Loans, and council housing schemes.

Request a Valuation

What is a local authority valuation?

State-backed lending schemes administered through local authorities have their own valuation requirements, and they are not always the same as a bank's. The Local Authority Home Loan and the Local Authority Purchase and Renovation Loan both require valuation reports in a prescribed format, and renovation lending typically requires more than one, a value before works and a projected value after them.

Getting the form or the basis wrong here is a costly mistake, because it usually means starting the loan assessment again.

  • Where a local authority valuation is required
  • Local Authority Home Loan applications
  • Local Authority Purchase and Renovation Loan applications, including pre- and post-works valuations
  • Vacant and derelict property grant schemes where a valuation is required
  • Tenant purchase and affordable purchase schemes
  • Local authority property acquisitions and disposals

Why the format matters

Local authority schemes are administered to defined rules. The valuation must be on the correct form, on the correct basis, and signed by a valuer with the required accreditation. Send us the scheme details and any form the council has issued, and we will work to what they have asked for.

What your valuation includes

  • Inspection of the property in its current condition
  • A report on the local authority's prescribed form where one is specified
  • Where the scheme requires it, both a current value and a projected post-renovation value
  • Supporting comparable evidence
  • A signed report from a PSRA-licensed, IPAV-registered valuer
Questions

Local authority valuation FAQs

I am buying a derelict property to renovate. What do I need?

Renovation lending generally requires a valuation of the property as it stands and an assessment of value once the proposed works are complete. Send us the schedule of works or the drawings with your enquiry and we will confirm what the scheme requires.

Which councils do you cover?

All four Dublin local authorities and the local authorities throughout Leinster.

The council has sent me a form. Can you complete it?

Yes. Send it with your enquiry and we will work to it directly.
